Notes about Abstract Algebra from Artin and Dummit-Foote.

- Symmetric Group $S_{n}$: Permutation of set of indices $\{ 1,2,\dots,n \}$.
	- Finite group of order $n!$
- Symmetric group of a set $T$, defined as $\text{Sym}(T)=\{ \text{all bijections }a:T\to T \}$ under composition $a\circ b(t)=a(b(t))$, where $a,b$ are bijections of the set.
	- This can be referred to as [automorphism group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphism_group) of T, written as $\text{Aut}(T)$
