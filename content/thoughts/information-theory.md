# Information Theory

> [!note] Prove Jensen's inequality, $f(E[X])\leq E[f(X)]$ for a differentiable convex function $f$ and random variable $X$.

> [!note] Prove KL-Divergence is non-zero, $D_{\text{KL}}(p\|q)\geq0$.
